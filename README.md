# Coffee-shop-sales
 
☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Internship Project

Project Overview:
Developed an interactive Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard using Power BI to analyze retail sales data and generate actionable business insights. The dashboard helps understand sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and store-level trends.

Key Analysis Performed:

Analyzed sales trends by day of the week and hour of the day.
Identified peak sales periods to understand customer purchasing patterns.
Analyzed monthly sales revenue and overall sales performance.
Compared sales performance across different store locations.
Calculated average order value per person.
Identified best-selling products based on quantity and revenue.
Analyzed product categories to understand their contribution to total sales.
Created interactive KPIs, charts, slicers, and tables for easy business reporting.

Tools & Technologies:
Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Cleaning | Data Visualization | Excel

Key Skills Demonstrated:
Data Analysis | Dashboard Development | Data Visualization | Business Insights | KPI Reporting
